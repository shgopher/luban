=== 4) 变更流水限长 + 7 天 TTL：防内存无限增长，过期后客户端回退全量快照 ===
$ redis-cli -h ::1 -p 16383 XADD sync:changes:u1001 MAXLEN ~ 10000 * kind msg conv c2 seq 777 msg_id m_c2_000777
1795242096555-0

$ redis-cli -h ::1 -p 16383 EXPIRE sync:changes:u1001 604800
1

$ redis-cli -h ::1 -p 16383 TTL sync:changes:u1001
604800

$ redis-cli -h ::1 -p 16383 EXPIRE sync:cursor:u1001:t_phone 604800

$ redis-cli -h ::1 -p 16383 TTL sync:cursor:u1001:t_phone
